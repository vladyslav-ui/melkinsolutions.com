The Problem

The Inspection Gap

Lethal & Expensive

Manual Entry is Failing

Confined space and underground operations cost lives annually. Mandated safety protocols, rescue standby teams, and massive asset downtime push traditional inspection costs above $122,000 per event. As a result, critical infrastructure operates dangerously below required compliance frequencies.

The Technical Wall

Single-Mode Robotics Fall Short

Standalone drones are agile but hit an endurance limit at 150 meters. Standalone rovers have the battery capacity to travel deep underground but suffer severe capability loss over rough terrain and cannot inspect vertical stopes. The industry requires a persistent, integrated solution.

The Solution

HAGES Architecture

A fully integrated, multi-modal robotic network engineered to shatter the endurance wall. The rover acts as a mobile base, transporting the UAV deep into the hazard zone for repeated, high-value sorties.

Ground Segment

A tracked crawler base built for uneven terrain and rocky inverts. It transports the aerial asset, houses the primary computing payload, and carries a massive 24V energy reserve.

Aerial Segment

An industrial quadcopter modified for turbulent underground airflows. It utilizes SLAM autonomy to navigate, avoid obstacles, and map complex vertical geometries without GPS.

Mid-Mission Recharge

The core breakthrough. The UAV autonomously lands on the rover, locks into place, and wirelessly recharges its batteries before launching deeper into the environment.
